1. Founding Purpose

The Korea Cooperative of Care Robot (KCCR) was established through autonomous, self-reliant, and cooperative efforts among robotics professionals and specialized enterprises to contribute to the advancement of national care robotics and welfare technologies.

Core Objectives & Background

  • Addressing the Super-Aging Society: Mitigating acute healthcare labor shortages and night-shift burdens while improving the quality of care for patients and the elderly.
  • Joint R&D & Rapid Commercialization: Overcoming individual SME limitations by uniting roboticists, healthcare institutions, and academic researchers to co-develop field-optimized technologies.
  • Building a Collaborative Industry Ecosystem: Standardizing core modular components (Omni-wheels, AI vision, healthcare HRI) and conducting joint marketing and procurement.

4. Key Business Activities

Night Nursing Rounding Robot

  • Autonomous & Agile Navigation: Omni-directional drive modules for tight ward spaces with smooth S-curve motion profile.
  • Low-Light Vision AI: IR and 3D ToF multi-modal sensor fusion for non-disturbing night patrol and fall detection.
  • Medical HRI & Vitals: On-device whisper-recognition AI (Moonshine), contactless vital sign estimation, and real-time EMR/OCS integration.

Mobility & Assistive Devices

  • Mobility & Walking Assist: Smart robotic wheelchairs, standing/repositioning assistive robots, and dual-arm harness walking trainers.
  • Specialized Care: Automatic excretion care robots and assisted showering mobile capsules.
  • Standardized Modular Parts: Low-cost, lightweight Omni-wheels co-applied across multiple member products.

Joint Marketing & Consulting

  • Joint Commercialization: Cooperative-level branding, public procurement registration, and global exhibition participation.
  • Field Demonstration & Training: PoC clinical trials in tertiary hospitals and specialized robot operational training.
  • Healthcare Innovation Consulting: Smart hospital workflow optimization and government policy recommendations.

5. Future Strategic Directions

Healthcare Big Data Platform

  • Transition from physical logistics to a 24/7 continuous vital signs and patient status analytics platform.
  • Development of AI-driven predictive medicine and early warning alert architectures.

Diversified Revenue Models

  • Transitioning from one-time hardware sales to integrated hardware + annual maintenance (SLA) + cloud AI SaaS subscriptions.
  • Monetization of core algorithm licensing and standardized component royalties.

Market Expansion & Global Leadership

  • Expanding from tertiary general hospitals to nursing homes, home healthcare, and infant care domains.
  • Targeting overseas markets (Japan, North America, Europe) based on domestic clinical references and international certifications (KC, IEC 60601, GMP).
