1. Founding Mission

The Korea Cooperative of Care Robots (KCCR) was established to contribute to the advancement of caregiving robots and welfare technology through autonomous, self-reliant, and cooperative activities.

Core Objectives & Background

  • Addressing Super-Aged Societies: Mitigating heavy physical workloads on healthcare workers and improving recipients' quality of life in response to rapid demographic aging.
  • Joint R&D & Commercialization: Fostering collaboration among academia, research institutes, hospitals, and robotics enterprises to develop and deploy field-tailored care robots.
  • Industrial Ecosystem Development: Standardizing core modular components (omni-wheels, AI vision, HRI) and conducting joint marketing.

4. Key Initiatives

Night Shift Nursing Rounding Robots

  • Autonomous Navigation in Narrow Wards: Omni-directional drive with smooth S-curve acceleration
  • Low-Light Vision Fusion: Low-light IR and 3D ToF vision fusion for patient monitoring and fall detection
  • Medical HRI & Vitals: On-device voice AI, non-contact vital sign monitoring integrated with EMR/OCS

Transfer & Custom Mobility Assistance

  • Mobility & Gait Training: Smart wheelchairs, standing/repositioning robots, dual-arm gait trainers
  • Specialized Care Solutions: Automatic toileting care robots and mobile shower-assist capsules
  • Standardized Core Modules: Lightweight, cost-effective omni-wheel modules

Joint Marketing & Consulting

  • Joint Branding: Unified distribution network, government procurement registration, exhibition pavilions
  • Demonstration & Training: PoC validation in tertiary hospitals and nursing homes
  • Care Innovation Consulting: Smart hospital process re-engineering and policy development

5. Future Directions

Healthcare Big Data Platform

  • Real-time continuous patient vital and condition analytics beyond basic item delivery
  • AI-driven early anomaly detection and predictive healthcare expansion

Business Model Diversification

  • Expansion from hardware sales to annual maintenance and cloud AI services (SaaS)
  • Commercialization of technology licensing and core standard components

Global Market Expansion

  • Broadening deployment to nursing homes, home healthcare, and pediatric care
  • Entering international markets (Japan, North America, Europe) with clinical references and certifications
