Care Robot 2026 Public Contests

To envision a harmonious future of caregiving and robotics technology, Care Robot 2026 is organizing a Drawing Contest and an Essay Contest. Winning entries will be exhibited and awarded during Care Robot 2026 on November 26.

🎨 "The Robot of My Dreams" Drawing Contest

  • Eligibility: Preschoolers and elementary school students
  • Artwork Requirements: Must feature at least one human and one robot
  • Media: No restrictions (crayons, watercolors, colored pencils, digital drawings, etc.)
  • Submission Deadline: Monday, October 12, 2026, 18:00 KST
  • Submission Methods (Choose 1):
    • On-site: Draw and submit directly at the Smart Life Week booth (Oct 6–8, COEX)
    • Online: Take a clear photo of the completed artwork and send it to kccrobot@gmail.com
  • Contact Person: Jihye Park (+82-10-4753-6580)

✍️ "My Caregiving Story" Essay Contest

  • Theme: Sincere essays reflecting personal experiences of giving or receiving care
  • Length: 3,000 to 5,000 characters (including spaces)
  • Submission Deadline: Monday, October 12, 2026, 18:00 KST
  • Submission Method: Email submission (kccrobot@gmail.com)
  • Contact Person: Heeok Yang (+82-10-8650-6994)
