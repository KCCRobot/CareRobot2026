Topics of Interest

Topics are not strictly limited to the areas below; all innovative and practical research related to care robotics is welcome.

🦾 Physical Care

  • Mobility & Transfer Assistance Robots
  • Wearable Assistive Exoskeletons & Devices
  • Feeding & Domestic Care Support Robots
  • Bathing & Toileting Care Assistance Systems
  • Pressure Sore Prevention & Postural Repositioning
  • Smart Monitoring & Remote Care Platforms
  • Upper & Lower Limb Rehabilitation and Occupational Therapy Robots
  • Physical Human-Robot Interaction (pHRI)
  • Soft Robotics for Safe Caregiving
  • Safety, Ethics, Standardization, Testing & Certification

🧠 Mental & Cognitive Care

  • Emotional Support & Companion Robotics
  • Cognitive Rehabilitation & Dementia Care Robots
  • AI-Based Personalized Care Systems
  • Social Robots & Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)
  • Pediatric Care Robotics & Developmental Disability Support
  • Smart Nursing Support & IoT-based Care Infrastructures
  • Autonomous Service Robots in Hospitals and Care Facilities
  • Fall Detection, Prevention & Emergency Response Systems
  • Caregiver Support & Care Burden Reduction

Submission Formats

• Regular Paper: Up to 4 pages (Oral or Poster presentation)

• Poster: 1-page summary
