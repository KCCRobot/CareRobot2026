Care Robot 2026 Contests

Public Contests for a Compassionate Future with Care Robots
📅 Submission Deadline: Oct 12, 2026, 18:00 KST 🏆 Exhibition & Awards: Nov 26, 2026

Overview

At Care Robot 2026, we are hosting a Drawing Contest and an Essay Contest to picture the future where humans and robots coexist and to share touching caregiving experiences.

Selected works will be exhibited and awarded during the main conference on Thursday, November 26, 2026. We look forward to your active participation.

Drawing Category

"The Robot of My Dreams" Drawing Contest

Express the warm robot of the future living together and assisting humans through the eyes of children.

  • Eligibility Preschoolers and elementary students
  • Requirements Must include at least one human and one robot
  • Media No limitations (crayons, watercolors, colored pencils, digital art, etc.)
  • Deadline Monday, October 12, 2026, 18:00 KST
  • How to Submit (Choose one of the following methods):
    • On-site: Draw and submit directly at the Smart Life Week venue (Oct 6–8, COEX, Seoul)
    • Online: Take a clear photo of the artwork and submit via email (kccrobot@gmail.com)
  • Awards On-site exhibition and award ceremony on November 26, 2026
📞 Contact Person: Jihye Park (+82-10-4753-6580)
Essay Category

"My Caregiving Story" Essay Contest

Share your candid personal experiences and heartfelt moments of giving or receiving care in an essay.

  • Theme True experiences from the perspective of a care provider or recipient
  • Word Count 3,000 to 10,000 characters (including spaces)
  • Deadline Monday, October 12, 2026, 18:00 KST
  • How to Submit Email submission (kccrobot@gmail.com)
  • Awards On-site exhibition and award ceremony on November 26, 2026
