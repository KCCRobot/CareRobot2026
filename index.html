Welcome to Care Robot 2026

Organized by the Korea Co-op of Care Robots (KCCR), this event serves as a premier collaborative platform showcasing key research achievements and industry trends in care robotics—a crucial solution for super-aged societies. The main conference will take place on Thursday, November 26, 2026.
