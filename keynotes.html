Keynote Speakers

Dr. Won-Kyung Song
National Rehabilitation Center (NRC), Korea
Talk Title
Recent Trends and Future Directions of Care Robots in Korea
Keynote 1

Dr. Won-Kyung Song is a leading researcher in rehabilitation and care robotics at the National Rehabilitation Center (NRC), Korea. He currently serves as Head of the Assistive Technology Research Division, Director of the Rehabilitation Robotic Translational Research Project, and Director of the User-Driven Care Robot & Service Demonstration R&D Center.

He received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from KAIST in 2001. Prior to joining NRC, he served as a Senior Researcher at Samsung SDI (2001–2008). Since 2008, he has spearheaded the translational development of robotic rehabilitation and assistive care devices, contributing significantly to practical robotics for persons with disabilities and the elderly.
