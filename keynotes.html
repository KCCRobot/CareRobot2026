Keynote Speakers

Dr. Won-Kyung Song
National Rehabilitation Center (NRC), Korea
Keynote Speaker

Dr. Won-Kyung Song is a leading researcher in rehabilitation and care robotics at the National Rehabilitation Center (NRC), Korea. He currently serves as Director of the Department of Rehabilitation Assistive Technology Research, Head of the Rehabilitation Robot Translational Research Program, and Director in charge of the Consumer-Centered Care Robot and Service Demonstration R&D Program at the NRC.

He received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from KAIST in 2001. Before joining the NRC, he worked at Samsung SDI (2001–2008) as a Principal Research Engineer in new process development. Since joining the NRC in 2008, he has led research and translational development of rehabilitation assistive robots and care robots, contributing to the advancement and practical application of robotic technologies for people with disabilities and older adults.
