Keynote Address and Invited Speakers Guide
📅 November 26, 2026 📍 Severance Building, Yonsei Foundation, Seoul

Keynote Address

Dr. Wonkyung Song
National Rehabilitation Center (NRC)
Presentation Title
Recent Trends and Future Directions of Care Robots in Korea
Keynote Address

Dr. Wonkyung Song is a leading researcher in the fields of rehabilitation and care robotics at the National Rehabilitation Center (NRC). He currently serves as the Head of the Rehabilitation Assistive Technology Research Division, Director of the Rehabilitation Robot Translation Research Project Group, and Director of the User-Centric Care Robot and Service Demonstration R&D Project Group at NRC.

He received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from KAIST in 2001. Prior to joining the NRC, he worked as a Senior Researcher at Samsung SDI (2001–2008), conducting research on new process development. Since joining the NRC in 2008, he has spearheaded research and translational development in rehabilitation assistive and care robots, contributing significantly to the advancement and commercialization of robot technologies for individuals with disabilities and the elderly.

Special Field Dialogue

Director Hyesook Cho (Ph.D. in Nursing)
Nursing Home Green Hill
Presentation Title
[Special Field Dialogue]: User-Centric Care Robots, Seeking Answers in the Field
Special Field Dialogue

Dr. Hyesook Cho holds a Ph.D. in Nursing and has served as the Director of Nursing Home Green Hill since July 2000. She has also been an Adjunct Professor in the College of Nursing at Ewha Womans University since March 2007.

She has dedicatedly driven the development of the domestic care and geriatric nursing sectors by serving in prominent leadership roles, including President of the Home Care Nurses Association under the Korean Nurses Association (2004.03–2008.03), President of the Korea Nursing Home Association (2008.03–2011.03), and President of the Gerontological Nursing Society under the Korean Nurses Association (2011.03–2013.03).

Special Lecture

Prof. Jongbae Kim (Ph.D.)
CEO, MLP Inc. & (Former) Professor, Department of Occupational Therapy, Yonsei University
Presentation Title
Transforming the Power Wheelchair into a Robot
Special Lecture

Professor Jongbae Kim served as a professor in the Department of Occupational Therapy at Yonsei University and Head of the Rehabilitation Engineering Division at the Advanced Rehabilitation Medical Technology Research Institute. He majored in Applied Statistics at the University of Pittsburgh (1980–1983), received his Master's degree in Industrial Engineering from KAIST, and earned his Ph.D. in Rehabilitation Science and Technology from the University of Pittsburgh in 2005.

After working as a researcher and assistant professor at the University of Pittsburgh, he served as the Director (Head) of the Research Institute and Director of the Rehabilitation Robot Translation Research Center at the National Rehabilitation Center. He has also served as an adjunct professor at the University of Pittsburgh and Yonsei University. Additionally, he serves as the President of the Korea Wheelchair Rugby Association and CEO of MLP Inc., making significant contributions to the fields of rehabilitation engineering and adaptive sports for individuals with disabilities.

Rewiring the Walk: AI-Powered Exoskeletons in Modern Rehab
Industry Talk

Expert in powered exoskeletons, wearable robotics, and international market development.

Key Interests: Rehabilitation, Mobility Assistance, and Active Aging

Mr. Masahiro Satono
WACOH-TECH Inc.
Presentation Title
“Robots That ‘Sense’ Force Are Transforming Elderly Care: Applications of Force Sensing Pioneered by DynPick®”
Industry Talk

Force is essential information for robots interacting with humans. The 6-axis force sensor "DynPick®" combines low cost and high reliability utilizing electrostatic capacitance technology, and is already widely used primarily in the manufacturing sector as Japan's top market share holder.

This presentation introduces the features and use cases of DynPick®, providing insights into its potential applications in care and welfare robotics and new market opportunities.
