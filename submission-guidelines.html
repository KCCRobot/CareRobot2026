ORAL PRESENTATION

1. Paper (Oral) Presentation Guidelines

  • 1
    Presentation Language: Presenters may deliver their presentation in either English or Korean.
  • 2
    Time Allocation: Each paper is allotted 12 minutes for presentation and 3 minutes for Q&A (15 minutes total). For smooth time management, a chime bell will sound 10 minutes after the presentation begins.
  • 3
    Advance Submission of Presentation Slides (PPT): Please submit your presentation file to the Secretariat via email (kccrobot@gmail.com) by the morning of Tuesday, November 24 (KST) so it can be pre-loaded onto the session laptop. Please submit the file strictly in PPT (.ppt / .pptx) format, not PDF.
  • 4
    Presentation Equipment Policy: Due to the tight session schedule, personal laptops are not permitted in order to prevent display connection errors or delays. All presenters must use the official presentation laptop prepared by the Secretariat.
POSTER PRESENTATION

2. Poster Presentation Guidelines

  • 1
    Printing & Mounting Support: All posters will be printed and mounted by the Secretariat, so poster presenters do not need to print or bring their own physical copies.
  • 2
    Display Completion Time: Poster mounting will be completed no later than 10:50 AM (KST) on the day of the conference.
  • 3
    Post-it Questions & Comments: Conference attendees may write questions or comments on the Post-it notes provided by the Secretariat and attach them to the bottom section of each poster.
  • 4
    Preparing Q&A Responses: Poster presenters are encouraged to check the Post-it notes (questions and comments) attached to their poster and prepare their responses ahead of the poster session.
  • 5
    Mandatory Attendance at Poster Session (P1 Session): During the Poster Session (P1 Session) from 16:00 to 16:30 (KST), presenters must stand by their posters to engage in Q&A with attendees. Please strictly observe the session schedule and ensure you are present.
  • 6
    Poster Removal Time: The Secretariat will begin taking down the posters starting at 17:00 (KST).
  • 7
    Collecting Removed Posters: Presenters who wish to keep their printed poster may pick it up on-site immediately after the Closing Ceremony.
